How SIGHTX protects hiring

Strict verification is built into the database rules.

1. Employer review

Companies submit their details and stay pending until an authorized SIGHTX administrator verifies them.

2. Job moderation

New vacancies are private until an administrator publishes them after review.

3. Safer applications

Applicants can only apply to published vacancies. Resume uploads will be enabled after private file storage is configured.

A verification badge means SIGHTX reviewed available evidence; it is not a guarantee that every future message or job offer is safe.
